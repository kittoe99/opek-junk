1. HOW TO USE THIS SCRIPT
====================================================================

Every call ends in one of three outcomes: a booked job, a logged quote
with a follow-up time, or a solved problem. If none of those happened,
the call isn't done.

OPEK AT A GLANCE
- Services: junk removal, property cleanouts, small local moves and moving labor, mattress disposal, dumpster rentals.
- Coverage: all 50 states, through vetted local providers.
- Hours: 7 days a week, 7 am to 8 pm.
- Arrival windows: morning 8–12, midday 12–4, evening 4–7.
- Pricing: upfront and item-by-item for junk. Moving is hourly. Online booking price includes a 10% discount.
- Booking: a $1 fully refundable deposit holds the spot and is applied to the balance.
- Support: (831) 318-7139 · Support@opekjunkremoval.com · opekjunkremoval.com

FIVE RULES THAT MAKE THIS CONVERT
1. Ask one question at a time. Short questions, then listen. Callers who talk more buy more.
2. Get name and phone first, on every call. Read the phone number back digit by digit.
3. Give a number fast. Quote the total in one sentence, then stop talking.
4. Always ask for the booking. After every price, ask for the date. Never end on "let me know."
5. Sound like the office, not a call center. Use "truck," "crew," "haul," "load."

CORE CALL FLOW
1. Greet — name, company, warm offer to help.
2. Capture — full name, then phone.
3. Identify — new job, existing order, or problem? Route to the right section.
4. Discover — ZIP, service, what's going, access, timing.
5. Quote — one clear number (or hourly rates for moves), plus the value.
6. Close — assumptive date question, then the $1 deposit.
7. Confirm and wrap — read back details, set expectations, log the call.

3. QUOTE REQUESTS
====================================================================

The goal is a booked date on this call. Discover fast, quote once, then ask for the date.

SHARED DISCOVERY (EVERY SERVICE)
1. "What ZIP code is this in?"
2. "And what are we helping with — junk removal, a move, a mattress, a cleanout, or a dumpster?"
3. "When were you hoping to get this done?" (Urgency tells you how hard to close.)
4. "Is it ground floor, or are there stairs or an elevator?"

--- JUNK REMOVAL AND PROPERTY CLEANOUTS ---

DISCOVER
"Walk me through what's going — just the big pieces first."
"Anything else? Bags, boxes, anything in the garage or yard?" (Always ask twice.)

PRICE IT (internal, don't read aloud)
- Each item has a set price (sofa $99, mattress $89, fridge $119, dresser $89, TV $69; unknown item $49).
- Extra units of the same item cost 85% each.
- Minimum order is $169 total — it's a floor, not an add-on.
- Quote the booking price: subtotal minus 10%.

Examples:
- 1 chair: subtotal $169 (minimum) → booking price $152
- 1 sofa + 1 mattress: subtotal $188 → booking price $169
- Sectional + 2 dressers + 5 bags: $159 + $165 + $216 = $540 → booking price $486

PRESENT IT
"Okay, for the sofa, the mattress and the boxes, you're at [price] all in — that's loading, hauling, and disposal, and it already has your 10% booking discount."
(Then stop talking. Let them respond.)

CLOSE
"I've got [day] morning or [day] afternoon open in your area. Which works better?"
"To hold it, there's a one-dollar deposit — fully refundable, and it comes off your total. Want me to text you the link, or would you like to book at opekjunkremoval.com/booking?"

LARGE OR UNCLEAR JOBS (whole house, hoarding, estate cleanout)
"For a job this size, a quick look gets you the most accurate price. I can set up a free in-home estimate — what day works?"
(Book at opekjunkremoval.com/in-home-estimate.)

--- LOCAL MOVES AND MOVING LABOR ---

NEVER give a total dollar figure for a move. Share hourly rates and the truck fee only.

DISCOVER (in this order)
1. "Is it loading and unloading, just one end, or rearranging inside the home?"
2. "Do you need us to bring the truck?" (Skip for rearranges.)
3. "How big is the place — studio, one, two, or three-plus bedrooms?"
4. "One helper or two?" (Recommend two for 2+ bedrooms: it's faster.)
5. "Stairs or elevator at either place? How many flights?"
6. "Any heavy items — piano, safe, treadmill, pool table?"
7. "Need any packing or furniture taken apart?"
8. "What's the drop-off address?"

PRESENT IT
"It's one-nineteen an hour for two helpers, or one-forty-nine an hour for three, plus a ninety-nine-dollar truck fee since you need the truck. Rearranges have no truck fee."

IF THEY ASK HOW LONG (only if asked)
"Rough estimate only — a two-bedroom with stairs usually runs around five hours, but it's not fixed. It depends on the day."
Internal guide: studio 2 h, 1-bed 3 h, 2-bed 4 h, 3+ bed 6 h. Add about 1 h for stairs (plus 1 per extra flight), heavy items, packing, or disassembly. One end only is about 70%; rearranges about 60%.

CLOSE
"Two helpers will get you in and out faster. Want me to lock in [date], morning window?"

Hazardous waste: chemicals, wet paint, gasoline, motor oil, asbestos, propane tanks, biological waste.
"We can't take those for safety reasons, but everything else on your list we can haul."

4. OBJECTION HANDLING
====================================================================

Use Acknowledge → Reframe → Ask again. Agree with the feeling, give one reason, then ask for the date a second time.

"THAT'S MORE THAN I EXPECTED."
"I hear you. That price covers the crew, the truck, the loading, and the dump fees — nothing extra on the day. And it already has 10% off. If we take the [smallest item] off, you'd be at [lower price]. Want to go with the full load or trim it?"

"I'M GETTING OTHER QUOTES."
"Smart move. When you compare, check if they include disposal fees and labor — ours does. And since the deposit is just a dollar and fully refundable, you can hold a spot now and keep shopping. Want me to lock in [day] so you don't lose it?"

"I NEED TO THINK ABOUT IT."
"Totally fair. Is it the price, the timing, or something else?" (Solve the real one.)
"How about I hold [day] with the one-dollar refundable deposit? If you change your mind, you can cancel free up to 72 hours before."

"CAN YOU DO IT CHEAPER?"
"The online price is already our best rate — that's where the 10% comes in. What I can do is help you trim the list so it fits your budget."

"I NEED IT DONE TODAY / TOMORROW."
"Let me check what's open near you right now. If we can get a crew there, I'll need to lock it in on this call. Ready to grab that spot?"
(Never promise a time until it's confirmed.)

"I'M NOT SURE EVERYTHING WILL FIT / MY LIST MAY CHANGE."
"No problem. The crew confirms the load when they arrive. If it's more or less than we discussed, they'll adjust the price before any work starts."

"ARE YOU GUYS LEGIT? WHO'S ACTUALLY COMING?"
"Good question. We match you with a vetted, insured local provider in your area. You'll get confirmation and can track your order at opekjunkremoval.com/track-order."

MOVING: "JUST GIVE ME A TOTAL."
"I'd rather not guess and have it be wrong on the day. You only pay for the hours used — one-nineteen an hour for two helpers or one-forty-nine for three, plus the truck fee. Three helpers usually finish bigger moves faster, which saves you money."

6. ORDER UPDATES AND BOOKING CHANGES
====================================================================

Look it up before you answer. Never guess a time, crew, or status.

FIND THE ORDER
"Happy to check. Is this the phone number on the booking?" (If not: "What number did you book with?")
"Do you have your order number handy? It starts with OPK." (Optional — phone is enough.)
Self-serve option: opekjunkremoval.com/track-order

READ THE STATUS BACK
- Booked: "We have your request for [date], [window]. We're matching you with a local provider now, and you'll get a confirmation."
- Confirmed: "You're confirmed for [date] in the [window] window."
- Scheduled: "Your crew is scheduled for [date], [window]. They'll reach out before they arrive."
- On the way: "Good news — your crew is on the way."
- In progress: "The crew is working on your job now."
- Completed: "That job shows as completed on [date]. How did everything go?" (Ask for a review if happy.)
- Cancelled: "That booking shows as cancelled. Want me to get you back on the schedule?"
- Not found: "I don't see a booking under that number yet. Let me take your details and get you set up now."

"WHERE'S MY CREW?" (RUNNING LATE)
"I'm sorry for the wait. Let me reach out to your provider right now and call you back within [15] minutes with an update. Is this number best?"

RESCHEDULE
"No problem at all. What date works better? I have [option A] or [option B]."
"Changes are free up to 72 hours before your arrival window." (Under 72 hours, see the fee below — offer a new date first.)

CANCELLATION (SAVE ATTEMPT FIRST)
"I can help with that. Can I ask what changed?"
- Timing → "Would moving it to [date] work instead? It keeps your price locked in."
- Price → "If we trim the list, we can get you to [lower price]. Want to try that?"
- Found someone else → "Understood. Just so you know, our price already includes disposal and labor."
- Still cancelling → "No problem. I've cancelled it." (State the fee if it applies.)

CANCELLATION FEES (from the Terms of Service)
- 72+ hours before the arrival window: free.
- Under 72 hours: $69 cancellation fee.
- Dumpster cancelled within 48 hours of drop-off: $150 dry-run fee.
- If Opek cancels: full refund of anything paid.

ADD OR CHANGE ITEMS
"Sure, what are we adding?" (Re-price and read the new total.)
"That brings you to [new price]. I'll update the order now."

7. SUPPORT ISSUES AND COMPLAINTS
====================================================================

For upset callers, use LEAP: Listen, Empathize, Apologize, Plan. Don't defend or explain until they've finished.

OPENING FOR AN UPSET CALLER
"I'm really sorry you're dealing with this. Tell me what happened — I'm going to take notes so I get it right."
(After they finish) "That's frustrating, and I'd feel the same way. Here's what I'm going to do…"

CREW NO-SHOW OR VERY LATE
"I'm sorry — that's not the experience we want for you. I'm contacting your provider now. I'll call you back within [15] minutes with a firm update or a new time."
If it can't be saved the same day: offer the first open slot and note the order for a goodwill review by a manager.

PRICE ON THE DAY WAS HIGHER THAN QUOTED
"Let's look at it together. The quote was for [items]. Did the crew add anything, or was the load bigger than we discussed?"
- Load was bigger → "That's why the price moved. The crew should have walked you through it before starting. I'm sorry that wasn't clear."
- Load was the same → "That shouldn't happen. I'm escalating it to our team today for review."

PROPERTY DAMAGE
"I'm sorry that happened. Let's get a claim started right away."
1. Get the order number, date, and what was damaged.
2. Tell them: "Please email photos and a short description to Support@opekjunkremoval.com within 48 hours of the job. That's required for the claim."
3. Explain: "Claims are reviewed under our SafeProtect platform protection. It isn't insurance, and it doesn't cover pre-existing damage or surfaces that weren't protected or cleared."
4. Never promise an amount or an outcome on the call.

SOMETHING WAS LEFT BEHIND / ITEM TAKEN BY MISTAKE
"Thanks for letting us know. What was it, and where was it? I'll contact your provider today and call you back with next steps."

REFUND REQUEST
- Deposit, job not done → "Your one-dollar deposit is fully refundable. I'll process that now."
- Opek cancelled the job → "You'll get a full refund of anything you paid."
- Job completed → "Our policy doesn't offer refunds for completed jobs. But I want to understand what went wrong — let me write this up for our team to review."

BILLING OR PAYMENT QUESTIONS
"Let me pull up the order. What's the charge you're seeing, and what date?"
(Confirm from the record. Never read full card numbers.)

ESCALATE TO A MANAGER WHEN
- The caller asks twice for a manager.
- Damage, injury, or a safety concern.
- A refund or credit beyond the deposit.
- A legal threat, or a chargeback is mentioned.

"I want to make sure this is handled right, so I'm bringing in my manager. They'll call you by [time]. Is this number still best?"

CLOSE EVERY SUPPORT CALL
"So to recap, I'm going to [action] and you'll hear back by [time]. Anything else I can help with?"
